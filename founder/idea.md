# BLVCK OAK — founder brief

Updated: 25 October 2026 (Australia/Sydney). This is a working strategy brief, not a claim of validated demand.

## What it is
- **Black Oak Digital / BLVCK OAK:** a Sydney-based digital agency offering website design, online booking/payment experiences, and related services for small businesses. Current website package catalogue: Essential **A$500**, Professional **A$1,500**, Enterprise **A$2,900** one-time. Link in Bio **A$13.13/month**. Source: [production setup](../SETUP.md).
- **BLVCK OAK Automate:** a distinct, early-stage product for website chat and email enquiry management. The pilot has a card-required seven-day trial concept, and *draft, not approved* Starter/Growth/Pro prices of **A$49/A$99/A$199 per month** with 500/1,500/4,000 AI requests. It is not production-launched and requires external integrations, privacy/terms approval, security verification and billing approval. Source: [Automate repository](https://github.com/SVMKVPV/BLVCK-OAK-AUTOMATE/blob/main/README.md).
- A broader marketplace and interactive demonstrations exist, but illustrative portfolio work does not count as proven customer outcomes.

## First customer hypothesis (unvalidated)
Owner-operated service firms in Sydney and surrounding areas that need a better online presence, struggle with incoming enquiries, or miss timely follow-up. Test one specific trade and business size first; do not assume this broad group behaves like one customer segment.

## Potential customer outcome
Make it easy for a prospective customer to enquire, and for the business owner to see, manage and follow up that enquiry reliably. Do **not** promise bookings, replies, revenue or automations that the current product cannot perform.

## Where and how
Online via blvckoak.com.au, previews, referrals, local discovery and direct outreach. The repeatability and cost of each channel have not been established.

## Known constraints and unknowns
- Founder delivery capacity, customer interviews, pipeline, paid conversion, customer acquisition cost, project completion hours and support burden are not provided.
- No verified cost-of-service, AI usage, monthly operating expense, retention or revenue cohort data; therefore the CFO verdict must remain **Incomplete**.
- Australian business identity, ABN/GST invoicing, final legal terms and refund/guarantee wording need confirmation before new live commercial promises. See [launch details](../BUSINESS-DETAILS-BEFORE-LAUNCH.md).
- Keep agency and Automate revenue, costs and launch gates separate. Do not switch Automate to live billing based on this document.

## Decision requested
Validate a narrowly targeted, outcome-led offer with real customers before expanding features, paying for mass advertising or promising production Automate service.
