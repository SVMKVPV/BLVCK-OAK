# BLVCK OAK — four-week validation sprint

Prepared **25 October 2026**. Proposed milestones, not booked appointments or real customer results. Owner: founder unless assigned otherwise.

## Week 1 — 26 October to 1 November
- Pick *one* niche, e.g. one local trade, using reachable prospects rather than hypothetical market size.
- Interview 15–20 owners: enquiry sources, response times, what is missed, current tools, purchase budget and objection to changing.
- Build an evidence table with date, consent, industry, problem frequency and verbatim notes; no fake testimonials.
- Identify five competitor/substitute choices with public sources and comparable pricing.
- Measure baseline agency funnel: visit source → enquiry → call → proposal → deposit, where data exists.

**Gate:** Select a niche based on actual pain and buyer accessibility; pause if the problem is not painful or buyers have no budget.

## Week 2 — 2 to 8 November
- Present one plain-language agency offer at an existing published price, with written inclusions, exclusions, revisions and delivery timeframe.
- Target 3–5 **paid, properly scoped agency pilots**, not promises of unverified revenue uplift.
- Quote and track delivery hours, contractor expenses, card fees, any affiliate payouts and post-launch support expectations.
- For Automate, invite a small number of private opt-in testers into the isolated test deployment only. Do **not** activate live email sending or payments.

**Gate:** Record actual proposals, deposit conversion and direct costs; no conclusion from interest alone.

## Week 3 — 9 to 15 November
- Deliver the pilots and observe the entire enquiry journey with informed participant consent.
- Record failed handoffs, AI uncertainty, human escalations, response time, support requests and workarounds.
- Confirm Automate's two-account isolation tests, domain/email handling, abuse protection, billing-webhook tests, terms and privacy gates against its [launch checklist](https://github.com/SVMKVPV/BLVCK-OAK-AUTOMATE/blob/main/docs/LAUNCH.md).

**Gate:** Do not launch paid Automate service until the real integrations and approvals pass.

## Week 4 — 16 to 22 November
- Compare paid demand with interview findings and present the real objections.
- Compute per-project contribution and agency capacity. Build a separate Automate cost-per-account/month and retention hypothesis from observed data.
- Choose one decision: double down on the winning niche, change the offer, or pause further feature work.
- Set a later launch date only after the critical production gates are complete.

## Tracking template
For each agency opportunity: date, channel, niche, proposed package price, deposit won (yes/no), direct labour hours and rate, contractors, payment and referral fees, support hours, reason for decision. For Automate: trial started, setup completed, active users, requests, provider expense, failures, human interventions, conversion and churn when legitimately measurable.

## Data needed from founder
1. Preferred first customer niche and past client mix.
2. Weekly hours and maximum budget for sales, delivery and ads.
3. Real historical enquiry, conversion, project cost, repeat-business and refund data.

No paid advertising budget, CAC ceiling, margin, acceptable price range or revenue forecast is asserted here because the necessary inputs have not been supplied.
