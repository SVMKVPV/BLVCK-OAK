# BLVCK OAK — first founder-board decision

Date: **25 October 2026**. Three independent framework reviews; *not* investment advice or opinions of the named thinkers.

**Vote: 2 FUND IF, 1 PASS. Average score: 4.0/10.** Offers 4/10 (FUND IF); Monopoly 3/10 (PASS); Product 5/10 (FUND IF).

The board supports **focused validation**, not broad feature expansion. This score is a judgment based on the supplied business evidence, not a measured probability of success.

## Shared risks
1. **Proof of paid demand is missing.** Portfolio demonstrations do not substitute for customer results, willingness to pay or retention data.
2. **Focus and defensibility are unclear.** Agencies and AI assistants compete in busy markets. A broad offer risks being compared on price.
3. **Unit economics and delivery reliability are unknown.** Labour, revisions, acquisition, provider costs, ongoing support and failure handling are not costed.

## Conditions to work through
- [ ] Pick one specific Sydney service-business category and validate its pain with 15–20 interviews.
- [ ] Identify and source five direct alternatives and substitutes; record comparable prices and complaints.
- [ ] Sell 3–5 properly scoped, paid agency pilot projects; record acceptance, delivery hours and direct costs.
- [ ] Observe the entire enquiry-to-owner-response flow with consenting pilot users, including failures and handoffs.
- [ ] Separate agency one-time project numbers from Automate subscription numbers.
- [ ] Calculate real project contribution and Automate provider cost, support, churn and customer acquisition before adopting a growth budget.
- [ ] Complete Automate's production integration, security, privacy, terms and billing approval gates before taking live subscriptions.
- [ ] Test a simple outcome-led offer and pricing against actual buyers.

## Strongest version of the business
Start with a narrowly defined service-business niche and deliver a reliable, measurable **website plus enquiry-capture/follow-up experience**. Use agency projects to learn real customer workflows. Automate becomes a separate paid subscription only after it safely delivers an outcome customers repeatedly choose to pay for. Keep the offer plain-language and the product narrowly scoped.

## What the lenses disagree on
The Offers and Product lenses see a conditional reason to test; the Monopoly lens passes because there is no proven moat or distribution advantage. A profitable local agency does not need a monopoly, but a scalable software ambition does need a credible reason for customers to choose and keep BLVCK OAK.
