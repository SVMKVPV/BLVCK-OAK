# Offers lens
Score: **4/10** | Vote: **FUND IF**

## What is strong
- Transparent agency prices and tangible demonstrations could reduce purchase uncertainty.
- Websites plus enquiry handling may provide a more useful outcome than selling page counts or AI usage.
- Automate could generate recurring revenue only if customers continue paying for demonstrated value.

## What would kill it
1. Prospects acknowledge missed enquiries but will not pay to solve them.
2. Website packages look interchangeable with other providers, forcing price competition.
3. Revisions, sales time, ongoing support, AI usage and acquisition costs make the offer uneconomic.

## Questions
- Which narrow industry experiences the most financially significant missed enquiries?
- What real evidence makes buyers believe the promised improvement?
- How quickly is the first result delivered, with what customer effort?
- Which guarantees are verifiable and affordable?

## If FUND IF: conditions
- Interview **15–20** prospective buyers in one segment and document problem frequency and willingness to pay.
- Sell **3–5 paid agency pilots** with a precisely scoped outcome; do not sell unready Automate functionality.
- Measure acquisition cost, delivery time, support, direct contribution and actual outcomes.
- Build the offer around a believable result and remove features that cost more than their customer value.
