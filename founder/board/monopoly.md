# Monopoly lens
Score: **3/10** | Vote: **PASS**

## What is strong
- A sufficiently narrow Sydney service-business niche may serve as an initial market.
- Agency relationships could become a differentiated customer-access channel for Automate.
- The founder has demonstrated ability to build interactive web experiences.

## What would kill it
1. Website creation and basic AI enquiry products are replicable; no demonstrated technological, brand, distribution or network advantage.
2. No known repeatable, affordable way to acquire the first 100 customers.
3. No measured reason for customers to stay when another agency or software vendor competes.

## Questions
- What *small* niche could BLVCK OAK serve unusually well?
- What is measurably better than the closest five alternatives?
- How will 100 clients arrive, and at what cost?
- What stops a copycat six months after launch?

## Reconsideration conditions
- Evidence of concentration and traction in one niche.
- A meaningful demonstrated advantage in enquiry workflow, speed, setup or results.
- Verified customer acquisition, support economics and retention.

*Framework: published ideas in Peter Thiel and Blake Masters' Zero to One, not a claim of their endorsement.*
