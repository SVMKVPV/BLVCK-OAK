# Product lens
Score: **5/10** | Vote: **FUND IF**

## What is strong
- Visual craft and a broad set of demonstration designs.
- Potential for one coherent customer experience: website enquiry → capture → owner awareness → follow-up.
- A simple, desirable owner experience could differentiate the business more than extra UI effects.

## What would kill it
1. Spreading effort across custom websites, marketplace and Automate before one workflow is exceptional.
2. Failed handoffs among a website, email, AI draft, human response and customer.
3. Billing tiers and AI quota language making a straightforward buyer outcome feel complicated.

## Questions
- What is the one thing the business must do brilliantly?
- What happens in the first five minutes after signup?
- What does the owner see when AI is uncertain or a provider fails?
- Which features can be removed until the core workflow is reliable?

## If FUND IF: conditions
- Focus the product hypothesis on **never losing track of a customer enquiry**, without guaranteeing results.
- Observe multiple real consenting test users through the complete journey.
- Validate account separation, safe human fallback, email, AI, privacy, billing and support before paid Automate rollout.
- Simplify the pitch and customer setup based on observed friction.
