Privacy policy.

How Black Oak Digital handles information submitted through this website, checkout and referral program.

Business identity

Black Oak Digital displays the following registered identifiers: ACN 701 878 775 and ABN 57 799 643 567.

Partner accounts and client quotes

Partner sign-in uses short-lived email codes and a secure session cookie. We store account details, hashed authentication tokens, client names and email addresses provided for quotes, service descriptions, approved prices, deposits and payment records. Partners can access their own quote records; the Black Oak owner can access all records to approve pricing and deliver services. Private quote links should be shared only with intended recipients.

Nearby lead finder and location

The nearby lead finder runs only when a signed-in partner selects “Use my location” and grants browser permission. The browser sends the current latitude, longitude, chosen distance and business category to a Black Oak server function, which sends the search area and category to Google Maps Platform. Black Oak does not save the coordinates or result list. We store only a per-account daily search count for cost control and abuse prevention. Google processes the search under the Google Privacy Policy. You can refuse or revoke location permission and continue using the rest of the partner workspace.

This operational policy should be reviewed against your registered business details and applicable Australian privacy obligations before live launch.

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, email, company, project message, referral registration and acceptance of referral terms. For referral integrity, we store a generated partner code, Stripe connected-account identifier, verification status and hashed customer email. We do not receive or store card numbers, passwords, identity documents or bank details.

Payments, Pay as You Sell and payouts

Stripe processes customer payments and referral-partner identity and bank information under Stripe’s own privacy terms. Black Oak receives transaction status, amount, package, limited customer contact details and identifiers needed to reconcile payments, prevent self-referrals and handle refunds, disputes and partner rewards. When a client chooses Pay as You Sell, they connect a Stripe account with read-only access. Black Oak stores the connected-account identifier and receives successful AUD payment amounts, payment identifiers, dates, refunds, disputes and disconnection events needed to calculate the agreed contribution. Black Oak does not receive the connected account’s password, bank details or authority to withdraw its sales balance. Sales tracking is disconnected automatically when the agreed website total is paid. If monthly maintenance is separately accepted, Stripe manages the subscription and Black Oak stores its status and identifier.

How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and deliver purchased services.
  • Verify referral ownership, attribute eligible sales and process rewards.
  • Prevent fraud, self-referrals, duplicate rewards and payment abuse.
  • Meet accounting, tax, dispute and legal obligations.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the website.

Service providers and overseas processing

The site uses Netlify for hosting and server functions, Stripe for payments and connected payouts, Resend for transactional email, and Google Maps Platform for partner-requested nearby business searches. These providers may process data outside Australia under their own safeguards and terms.

Retention and security

We retain records only as long as reasonably required for service delivery, referral administration, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Access is limited through private service credentials. No internet system is risk-free, so report suspected misuse promptly through the contact page.

Your choices

You may request access to or correction of personal information, ask a privacy question, or request deletion where retention is not legally required. Use the secure contact form and identify the email used for your enquiry or referral profile.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website, referral program or service providers change. The date above shows the latest published version.
