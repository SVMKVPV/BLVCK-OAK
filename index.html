Referral reward terms +

Rewards apply to successful, fully paid and non-refunded purchases attributed to an active referral code. A referred Link in Bio customer receives the first month free, then pays AUD $13.13 monthly until cancelled; no partner reward is created from the free month. Each other qualifying sale earns AUD $100. When the paid amount exceeds AUD $501, the reward is AUD $100 plus 10% of the full paid amount. For the limited Enterprise referral offer, the customer receives 60% off the AUD $2,900 list price and the partner reward is AUD $390: the AUD $100 base reward plus 10% of the original AUD $2,900 list price. The Enterprise offer is limited to the first 1,000 completed Stripe redemptions. Eligible rewards enter a minimum 14-day fraud and refund hold, then are processed in the next weekly payout run. Self-referrals, duplicate claims, fraudulent transactions, disputes, chargebacks and refunded payments are excluded or reversed. Completed Stripe identity and payout verification is required before a code can be used.
