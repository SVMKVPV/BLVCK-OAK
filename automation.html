A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
begin.

Prefer a conversation? Book a call ↗
Do I need a new website?

Often, no. A focused workflow can connect tools you already use. If a new page, portal or application is needed, we include that in the written scope.

Are the examples live products?

The examples on this page are interactive illustrations using sample data. They do not connect to real accounts, send messages or process payments. They show what we can scope and build, not off-the-shelf products already running for you.

Is the instant estimate my final price?

No. Focused workflows use a starting planning range. Complex integrations and full software products require discovery. We review overlap between selections and confirm the final quote before you commit.

What are the ongoing costs?

Depending on your solution, you may pay for hosting, third-party subscriptions, AI usage, SMS, phone calls or ongoing support. We identify these separately in your written quote. They are not included in this calculator.

Can I keep using my existing tools?

Where supported integrations and account permissions allow, yes. Tell us which tools you use. We check compatibility and any provider restrictions before confirming the solution.

Will AI send things without my approval?

We agree the approval rules with you. Drafting, checking and notifying can be automated while sensitive decisions and external messages stay with a person. The examples show review points rather than assuming unrestricted sending.

Can I start small or use a payment plan?

Yes, you can scope one useful workflow first. You can also request staged payments or discuss a 15-part plan. Availability, amounts and timing are confirmed in your written agreement.
